About Us

More than a meal — a tradition.

At Biriyani Kada, we believe biryani is more than just a meal — it's a tradition. Rooted in the rich culinary heritage of Kerala, we specialise in authentic dum biryani crafted with the finest long-grain basmati rice, hand-picked spices, and tender, slow-cooked meats.

Every plate we serve is prepared fresh, in small batches, using time-honoured recipes that balance aroma, flavour, and texture to perfection. From our signature chicken, mutton, beef, egg, and vegetable biryanis to traditional Kerala sides like salna, chutneys, and pickles, each dish is a celebration of Malabar's vibrant food culture.

Whether you're craving a quick, comforting lunch or planning a special family daawat, Biriyani Kada brings the warmth of a home-cooked biryani to your table — fresh, generous, and full of flavour, every single time.

“Cooked with love. Served with pride.”

  • Authentic dum process, sealed & slow-cooked
  • Finest long-grain basmati & kaima rice
  • Traditional Kerala sides — salna, chutneys, pickles
